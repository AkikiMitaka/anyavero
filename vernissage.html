Join us for the opening night of Women Groundbreakers x Jane Goodall Institute Austria - a truly special evening with Jane Goodall Institute ambassadors, inspiring guests, and a tribute to the one and only Jane Goodall.

The evening features a presentation on Jane Goodall's life and legacy, followed by a charity sale. Proceeds from sold artworks go towards the Jane Goodall Institute's Clever Girls project in Uganda - given the right opportunities, girls can break the cycle of poverty in just one generation.

Come and meet incredible people, learn about female role models, and share an evening of meaningful conversations.
