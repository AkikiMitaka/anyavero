Women Groundbreakers x Jane Goodall Institute Austria

Parents with Kids Workshops

Saturday 9 & Sunday 10 May 2026

Dates
Saturday 9 & Sunday 10 May 2026
Sessions
4 sessions across the weekend (see below)
Venue
Inside Out Gallery
Kirchengasse 33, 1070 Vienna
Admission
“Meet a Scientist” drop-ins free · Drawing workshops 20 EUR per child (parents free)

The four sessions

  • Sat 9 May · 12 – 2 pm Meet a Scientist (drop-in, free)
  • Sat 9 May · 3 – 5 pm Parents & children drawing session (register, €20)
  • Sun 10 May · 12 – 2 pm Meet a Scientist (drop-in, free)
  • Sun 10 May · 3 – 5 pm Mother's Day drawing session (register, €20)

A weekend of creative workshops for parents and children, set among the portraits of Women Groundbreakers.

"Meet and greet a scientist" - an introduction to the women behind the discoveries: Marie Curie, Lise Meitner, Rosalind Franklin, Ada Lovelace and more. Hands-on, age-appropriate, and fun.

Mother's Day special - drawing session - children and mothers drawing each other and learning about female role models, on the weekend of Mother's Day.

Choose the slot that works best for your family when you register. “Meet a Scientist” drop-ins are free; drawing workshops are 20 EUR per child, with accompanying parents admitted free. Open to all ages; children must be accompanied by an adult.
